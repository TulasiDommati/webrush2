01Journey

The journey

Monthly activity across all three sources, from the first recorded play in July 2013 to the last in December 2024. Markers indicate the key events on which the chapters are based. Select a marker or a month to open that period in the explorer.

Scroll horizontally to pan the chart.

02Chapters

Chapters

The eleven-year record is divided into eight periods, with boundaries placed where the data changes character. All figures are computed from the underlying records at load time. The threads under each chapter join records from different sources on the same day, for example a purchase and the music playing at the time.

Scroll horizontally or drag the bar to move between chapters.

03Rhythms

Rhythms

Aggregate patterns across the full record: the hours and weekdays when listening and spending occur, the shift from cash to digital payment, changes in listening breadth over time, and recurring purchases.

04Places

Places

The expense tracker anonymises locations as "Place 0", "Place 1" and so on, but the routes between them can be reconstructed from transport entries. The card statement records a city and state for each transaction; close to half of those transactions are flagged by the bank as fraudulent and are marked as such throughout.

05Explorer

Explorer

A searchable index of every record across the three sources. Select a record to view related activity from the same day, together with its history across the dataset.

Your life,in receipts.

Method. Three exports were treated as one person's: a Spotify streaming history (UTC, shifted to IST), a household expense tracker (INR, Mumbai), and a card statement. Card rows without a date or amount were dropped; its coordinate columns were discarded because they span the whole globe (synthetic noise). The fraud flag is shown exactly as the bank recorded it. Everything on this page is computed in the browser from data/data.json — no backend.
